Course Layout

Agate Beach is a 3,002-yard, Par 36 course that offers a challenging yet accessible layout for all skill levels. Famous for our signature 8th hole, we provide a serene park-like setting protected by shore pines.

About Us

Agate Beach Golf Course was designed by Frank Stenzel Sr. in 1931. It started as a humble layout on the Oregon coast and has grown into a community staple.

Since 1960, the Martin family has been the steward of this beautiful course. Our mission is to provide an inclusive, friendly, and high-quality golf experience for locals and visitors alike.
